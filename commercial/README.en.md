# Commercial License Template Pack

[中文](README.md) | **English**

> These are operational and drafting templates, not legal advice, and they are not an effective license by themselves. Have a lawyer familiar with software licensing, guardianship, and local tax rules review them before signature. Replace every bracketed field; an incomplete template must not be sent to a customer.

## Recommended structure

When the author is a minor, use this structure:

- **Author / copyright owner**: the author
- **Licensor / contracting party**: the author's guardian acting as legal representative; if the guardian already operates a business, that sole proprietorship or company may act as licensor
- **Payment, invoicing, and taxes**: the licensor
- **Customer**: the individual, company, or organization buying the commercial license

Do not register a sole proprietorship with the minor as operator, and do not describe the guardian as the author or transfer copyright to the guardian without separate legal advice. The contract, merchant account, invoice, and license certificate must use the same contracting party.

## Files

- `guardian-authorization.template.en.md`: internal confirmation that the guardian may act for the author
- `commercial-license-agreement.template.en.md`: commercial license framework
- `order-form.template.en.md`: scope, fees, and payment order form
- `license-certificate.template.en.md`: one-page customer certificate
- `first-reply-email.template.en.md`: first response to a commercial inquiry
- `license-registry.template.csv`: license and renewal register

## Issuance sequence

1. Confirm the customer, use case, scale, modification/redistribution/white-label/hosting needs, and term in a private channel.
2. Complete the order form and quote; state that a quote is not a license.
3. Both parties sign the commercial license agreement and confirm the guardian or business-entity authority chain.
4. Collect payment, issue the agreed invoice or receipt, and handle taxes.
5. Send the PDF certificate, signed agreement, and delivery record. If private repository access or updates are promised, provision them separately.
6. Record the license number and renewal date. Do not put customer personal data in the public repository.

## Certificate format

- Keep the source template in Markdown for review and version control.
- Use **PDF** for the customer-facing certificate: the layout is fixed, it can be signed or stamped, supports digital signatures, and is easier to archive than an image.
- If a Word workflow is required, keep a `.docx` editable master but export the final delivery to PDF.
- Do not use JPG or PNG as the only certificate; images are harder to search, verify, and preserve.

The certificate is a summary. The signed commercial license agreement controls.
